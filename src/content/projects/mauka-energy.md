## Abstract

This Oregon State consulting project examined the commercialization of Mauka Energy’s routing tools for heavy-duty electric vehicles. The team connected the technical opportunity in regenerative braking and energy-aware routing to customer segments, pricing, distribution, and financial planning.

## Context

Heavy-duty fleets operate under route, terrain, energy, and service constraints. A technically promising routing method still needs a credible path to customers. The consulting work considered how energy recovery and route selection could be positioned for fleet operators and industry partners.

## My role

I participated in the Oregon State University consulting team in spring 2025. The published page attributes the commercialization work to the team. It does not establish sole ownership of the routing algorithm, patents, or every analysis task.

## Approach

The project page presents stakeholder and customer analysis, market segmentation, a proposed tiered SaaS offering, and an implementation roadmap. Suggested segments include port logistics, utility services, airport ground support, and municipal fleets with stop-dense operations.

## Implementation

The implementation deliverable is a commercialization presentation and a supporting project site. The proposed rollout connects technology validation, beachhead-market definition, pilot partners, a future web platform, and eventual integrations with OEM and telematics partners.

These roadmap stages are proposals. A dashboard, routing API, cloud platform, or pilot deployment is not demonstrated by the presentation repository.

## Observations and outcomes

The public record establishes a completed strategy presentation and an organized commercialization roadmap. It includes market-size and revenue projections, which are scenario assumptions rather than realized financial results. This case study deliberately avoids using those projections as performance claims.

## Limitations

Commercial feasibility depends on validation, customer adoption, partnerships, and implementation work beyond a classroom consulting engagement. No realized energy savings, revenue, pilot results, or product launch are asserted here.
